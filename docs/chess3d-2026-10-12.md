# Chess(3d) implementation and verification

## Scope

Chess(3d) is a separate entry next to Chess in plugin and web/mobile game lists.
The original 2D game remains unchanged. Both clients lazy-load the shared
Three.js renderer in `packages/core/components/Chess3DBoard.tsx`.

The supplied reference informed the walnut/maple board, natural light pieces,
polished dark pieces, Staunton silhouettes, straight elevated perspective,
yellow-green square highlights. Board textures are seeded local bitmaps.
The current room uses actual Three.js furniture and architecture, scanned
walnut/leather/plaster/parquet materials, and a modeled U1-style upright. The piano
is near the center of the rear wall. Dragging orbits the entire room, wheel
and pinch zoom, and icon controls reset the view, look overhead or zoom.
Orbit bounds keep the camera above the desk; panning is disabled. Camera
changes do not submit chess moves. Shadows are refreshed when pieces move,
not on every camera movement. All runtime assets are bundled locally.

The previous fixed-view release used a photograph, preserved as a mood
reference in `packages/core/assets/chess-table-room.webp`. The built-in image
generation tool created that photograph with this prompt:
"Photorealistic dark warm music room, authentic upright piano on the right,
warm wall lamp on the left; generously wide but shallow tall matte dark walnut
writing table in the foreground, empty central tabletop; a real curved walnut
chair with charcoal leather upholstery opposite the seated viewer; centered
elevated perspective, subtle grain, believable lighting, no chessboard,
pieces, people, text, plastic or toy geometry." The original source is
`/Users/jasonpark/.codex/generated_images/019ef57c-0909-7363-88f8-5f655993f3ac/exec-ad1a2100-234b-4743-81e6-526bc7dcc7cf.png`.
Earlier `chess-room.webp` / `chess-walnut.webp` assets remain preserved but are
no longer loaded by this renderer.

The following verification/deployment entries describe the earlier reference

Existing chess room rows keep `game_type: chess` and store the variant in
`captured.presentation: 3d`. Creation, moves, rematches and resume preserve it;
invites identify `chess_3d`. Existing multiplayer, AI and promotion flows are
reused. Legal 3D targets and check highlighting use chess.js. WebGL loss or
absence falls back to the playable 2D board. Hidden panes pause rendering;
unmount disposes geometry, textures, shadows and the GPU renderer.

## True 3D Room and U1 Exterior Release

The user superseded the fixed-photo view with an orbitable real 3D room,
then requested a higher-quality piano modeled from Yamaha U1 drawings.
`chess3dRoom.ts` constructs the architecture, beveled walnut desk, upholstered
chair, piano bench, curtains and lamp. No backdrop photograph is rendered.
The old images remain preserved as references. A shared metre scale (ten
world units per metre) keeps the 1.85 m desk, 0.49 m chair and piano consistent.

The piano is original geometry in `chess3dPiano.ts`, based on Yamaha's public
[U1 front/side dimension drawing](https://www.yamaha.com/us/pianos/images/u1linenew.jpg)
and [official exterior photograph](https://www.yamaha.com/us/pianos/images/U1-1-c6f7e2c3.jpg).
The selected drawing specifies 153 cm width, 121 cm height and 62 cm depth;
regional brochures sometimes list 61 cm. This is an exterior visualization,
not an internal manufacturing CAD or a claim of Yamaha affiliation.
Unpublished part dimensions are estimated from the exterior references.
Yamaha photographs, diagrams and proprietary models are not bundled.

The model includes 52 individually notched white keys, 36 instanced black
keys in the A0-to-C8 sequence, curved keybed cheeks, slender front legs,
casters, three curved brass pedals, separate cabinet panels, an open
fallboard and folded music rest. Polished ebony uses a one-time reflection
capture of the actual warm room and broad soft lighting, not a flat black
material. The capture and scene resources are disposed when unmounted.
Room PBR maps are locally bundled CC0 assets from Poly Haven; provenance is
in `packages/core/assets/chess-room3d/sources.json`. The preparation script
checks source MD5 hashes. There are no runtime third-party asset requests.

## Furnished Room, Lights and Original Piano Refinement

The imported Tall3 piano was tried and explicitly rejected by the user.
It is not bundled or rendered, and its attribution control was removed.
The original Slur U1-inspired geometry remains, with tapered black-key sides,
smooth 49-ring brass pedals, a cabinet lid lip and light-responsive lettering.
The piano and bench now share X=12.5 (45 cm farther right). The sofa,
side table and its lamp moved 20 cm away from the window together.

The room now uses locally bundled CC0 Poly Haven furniture/pieces, listed in
the adjacent `*-source.json` files. The chair retains the approved chocolate
brown treatment. Wall/table lamps are independently clickable in the scene;
emitter positions are measured after world transforms, and extinguishing the
wall lamp also turns off its auxiliary reflection light. No toolbar lamp remains.
Two brass candles mark the active player's right-hand side. Reduced motion
keeps the flame steady; hidden/unmounted games stop animation and sound.

Lobby/game camera distances are 34/23.5 with a fixed board-centred pivot.
Starting a match reuses the renderer and materials, preventing white flashes.
The sound button defaults to muted and remembers the local preference.
The bundled CC0 wood-piece placement sample plays once per real move;
3D chess suppresses the old synthesized game sounds without altering 2D chess.
